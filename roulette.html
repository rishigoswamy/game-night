Rules

  • Betting: drag a chip onto a space, or select a chip and click the space. To take a bet back, drag your chip off the table (or right-click / long-press the space). Drag it onto another space to move it.
  • Phones at the table: this computer's table is the master. While a Casino-style game runs here, guests on the same Wi-Fi open this computer's address on their phone (shown under 📱 in the top bar) and join automatically with their own players (up to 6 seats in all). Each phone controls only its own players; this screen controls only players added here. With phones connected the wheel spins only when the betting window closes. 📱 marks phone players and dims if their phone disconnects (their players are kept).
  • Open table: "Open the table now" on the setup screen starts an empty casino table that never ends on its own. People join with "+ Add player" and leave with "Cash out" whenever they like, and the wheel keeps spinning in between (30-second betting windows unless you picked another time).
  • 0 and 00: when the ball lands on a green pocket the whole screen celebrates (cash and all) and the table groans. When a hot number comes in again, the table cheers. When anyone wins $500 or more on one spin, it's a big-win celebration with a long hurray. See them any time:
  • Flush history: Recent Numbers is kept on this device across games and restarts. The 🔒 Flush button erases it after an admin check: the device's own unlock (Touch ID / fingerprint / Face ID, on https or localhost) or the admin PIN. The lock is set up on the Admin page, linked from the lobby.
  • Cash out: during betting, tap "Cash out" on your card to leave with your current balance (chips you bet this round are returned first). Your total counts in the final standings. The others keep playing; the game ends when everyone has cashed out or gone bust.
  • Casino style (default): the dealer opens a betting window and everyone bets at once; tap a name above the table to bet as that player. When the window closes (or someone taps "No more bets") the wheel spins, and 5 seconds after the result the next window opens. Use "+ Add player" to join mid-game (up to 6).
  • Turns style: 2–6 players bet one after another. When the last player is done, betting closes and the wheel is spun as the final step; with a time limit on it spins by itself after 5 seconds. The first bettor rotates each round. Only the betting player's chips and results are shown.
  • Payouts: single number (incl. 0 and 00) 35:1 · dozen or column 2:1 · red/black, odd/even, 1–18/19–36 1:1.
  • Zeros: 0 and 00 are green — every outside bet loses when either comes up.
  • Time limit: each player has a set time to bet, chosen at setup: 15 seconds to 3 minutes, or a custom number of seconds (1 minute by default). When it runs out, the turn passes to the next player. Once all bets are in, the wheel spins by itself after a 5-second countdown (or press Spin the wheel to go sooner). The result window then moves on to the next round after 6 seconds, so with "No round limit" the table keeps running on its own and collects hot numbers. With no time limit, you spin and continue when ready.
  • Winning: most chips after the last round wins. Go broke and you're out; the game also ends when only one player is left.
  • Pause / Exit: use the buttons in the top bar. Pausing freezes the wheel, timer and dealer. Exiting ends the game and returns to setup.
  • Keys: Space = done / spin · Enter = next round · P = pause / resume · Esc = resume / close rules.

Payout Table

BetWhere to place itNumbers coveredPays ProfitReturnedChance to win
  • Profit is what you win on top of your bet. Returned is profit plus your original chips, which come back to you on a win.
  • The wheel has 38 pockets (1–36, 0 and 00). 0 and 00 are neither red nor black, odd nor even, low nor high, and aren't in any dozen or column, so every outside bet loses when one comes up.
  • You can place several bets in one round and each is paid separately. Example: $10 on 17 and $10 on Black, ball lands on 17 (black): $360 + $20 = $380 back.
  • Every bet has the same house edge of 5.26% (2 ÷ 38), so on average no bet beats another. They only differ in risk.
