Rules

  • Goal: beat the dealer by finishing closer to 21 without going over. You each play against the dealer, not against each other.
  • Card values: 2–10 count as shown, J/Q/K count 10, an Ace counts 1 or 11 (shown as e.g. "7 / 17").
  • Players: 2–6. Only the player who is betting, playing or being paid is shown on the table; the strip above the seat shows everyone's bet or hand at a glance.
  • Betting: players bet in turn. Tap a chip to add it to your bet, or drag it onto your seat. Drag your bet off the circle to take it back. Minimum bet $5.
  • The deal: everyone gets two cards face up; the dealer gets one up and one face down. With an Ace or 10 showing, the dealer checks for blackjack first.
  • Your turn: Hit takes a card · Stand keeps your total · Double doubles your bet for exactly one more card · Split a pair into two hands (one split per hand; split Aces get one card each).
  • Dealer: reveals the hidden card, then must hit until 17 or more and stands on all 17s.
  • Payouts: blackjack (Ace + 10-value as first two cards) pays 3:2 · a win pays 1:1 · a tie is a push (bet returned) · going over 21 is a bust and loses straight away.
  • Time limit: each bet and each decision has the time chosen at setup (15 seconds to 3 minutes, or a custom number of seconds). If it runs out, betting passes on, or your hand stands.
  • Cash out: during your betting turn, tap "Cash out" on your seat to leave with your balance (any bet not yet dealt is returned). Your total counts in the final standings; the others keep playing until everyone has cashed out or gone bust.
  • Winning: most chips after the last round wins. Go broke and you're out; the game also ends when only one player is left.
  • Pause / Exit: buttons in the top bar. Pausing freezes the timer and dealer.
  • Keys: Space = pass / deal · H hit · S stand · D double · X split · Enter = next round · P = pause · Esc = resume / close rules.

Payout Table

OutcomeWhen it happensPays ProfitReturnedHow often
  • Profit is what you win on top of your bet. Returned is profit plus your original chips. "How often" is a rough figure per hand with good play.
  • Each hand is settled on its own. A split hand that makes 21 with two cards is not a blackjack and pays 1:1.
  • If the dealer has blackjack, every hand loses except a player blackjack, which pushes.
  • You can double on any first two cards, including after a split (except split Aces). Doubling or splitting needs enough chips to match your bet.
  • The shoe is shuffled when about a quarter of it is left.
