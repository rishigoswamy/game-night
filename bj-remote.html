Rules

  • The table: Blackjack runs on the host computer. This phone joins with your own players; you bet and play your own hands from here.
  • Goal: finish closer to 21 than the dealer without going over. 2–10 count as shown, J/Q/K count 10, an Ace counts 1 or 11.
  • Your players: add up to 6 seats (shared with everyone) with + Add player; switch with the tabs. Only this phone can bet and play for them.
  • Betting: tap chips to add them to the selected player's bet (minimum $5). The cards are dealt for everyone when the betting window closes.
  • Your turn: this phone switches to that player and buzzes. Hit takes a card · Stand keeps your total · Double doubles the bet for exactly one more card · Split a pair into two hands. If the timer runs out, the hand stands.
  • Dealer: reveals the hidden card, then draws to 17 and stands on all 17s. With an Ace or 10 showing, the dealer checks for blackjack first.
  • Payouts: blackjack 3:2 · win 1:1 · tie = push (bet back) · over 21 = bust. Details in the Payout Table.
  • Cash out: while bets are open, leave with the player's balance (an undealt bet comes back first).
  • Connection: if this phone drops off, your players stay; reopen this page to carry on.
